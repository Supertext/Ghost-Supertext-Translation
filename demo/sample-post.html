Supertext translates your posts and pages straight from Ghost Admin. Add a tag such as #translate-de-ch, save, and a few seconds later a German draft is waiting in your posts list.

How it works

  1. Open a post and add a translate tag in the post settings.
  2. Save the post. Supertext translates the title, the excerpt and the whole body.
  3. Review the draft, adjust what you like, and publish it.

What stays the same

Formatting, links, images and cards are kept. Only the words change, so your layout looks exactly the same in every language.

💡
Translations are created as drafts, so nothing goes live before someone has checked it.
Good translations read as if they had been written in that language.
